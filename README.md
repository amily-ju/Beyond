## **为什么我们希望建立本社团 Why do we want to establish this club**

⾃闭症（孤独症）在当今⼉童群体中的现象⽇益普遍。由于沟通能⼒的局限，这些孩⼦往往被误解，甚⾄被排斥在群体之外。

对⽩社旨在为这些孩⼦创造直接与他⼈互动的机会，通过戏剧活动创造积极的体验，并帮助他们练习沟通技巧。

我们希望创办这个社团，是因为我们期待为同学们提供⼀个有意义的平台，利⽤创意与沟通来回馈社会。

通过与外部公益机构合作，我们希望让同学们接触到与我们不同，且处于相对弱势的群体，给同学们⼀个机会去理解并与他们互动。

同时，我们坚信，这个社团不仅能助⼒⾃闭症孩⼦们，也能让参与其中的同学在服务社会的过程中，培养同理⼼、团队协作精神与⾃信⼼。

Autism is a growing phenonmenon among children right now. These children are generally misunderstood and excluded from groups because of their lack of communication skills. 

Duibai Dialogue Club (对⽩社) aims to help these children by giving them opportunities to interact directly with others and use theatrical activities to create positive experiences and practice communication for these autistic children. 

We want to establish this club because we hope to create a meaningful opportunity for students to use creativity and communication to support the community. 

This club, in partnering with this outside organization, wants to show students children who are different than us, less fortunate than us, and give them a chance to understand and interact with them. 

We also believe this club can help students develop empathy, teamwork, and confidence while contributing to a meaningful cause.

## **活动目标 Goals**

我们希望营造⼀个积极且友好的环境，让同学们能够通过戏剧与表演活动与⾃闭症⼉童建⽴连接。

我们希望社团成员能够借此学习如何更有效地沟通、与他⼈协作，并提升对社区内多元需求的认知。

这些活动能在充满乐趣与⽀持的氛围中，帮助⾃闭症⼉童建⽴⾃信、表达⾃我并提升社交互动技巧；同时也让 YKPS 的学⽣们学会换位思考，传播关爱与意识。

Through opening this club, We hope to create a positive and welcoming environment where students can connect with children with autism through theater and performance activities. 

We want club members to learn how to communicate more effectively, collaborate with others, and become more aware of different needs within the community. 

The activities can help the autistic children build confidence, self-expression, and social interaction skills in a fun and supportive setting; while also teaching YKPS students about empathy and spreading awareness.

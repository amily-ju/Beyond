    <!-- ===== Module Modal 1: Metaphor's Crack ===== -->
    <div class="modal-backdrop member-modal" id="metaphorCrackModal" onclick="closeModuleModalOnBackdrop(event, 'metaphorCrack')">
        <div class="modal" role="dialog" aria-modal="true">
            <div class="modal-header" style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #FFF0F5 0%, #FFFFFF 100%); border-bottom: 3px solid #E8336A;">
                <button class="modal-close" onclick="closeModuleModal('metaphorCrack')" aria-label="Close">&times;</button>
                <p class="modal-title" style="color: #C01A52;">Metaphor's Crack</p>
                <p class="modal-title-cn cn" style="color: #E8336A;">隐喻的裂隙</p>
            </div>
            <div class="modal-body module-detail-body">
                <div class="module-detail-intro">
                    <p>Human conversation is woven with metaphors, pleasantries, and unspoken meanings. This tacit linguistic code can become a crevice too wide to cross.</p>
                    <p class="intro-cn cn">人类的对话遍布隐喻、客套与言外之意。这套心照不宣的语言密码，却会成为彼此之间难以跨越的裂隙。</p>
                </div>

                <div class="module-detail-hero">
                    <img src="images/module-metaphor-crack.jpg" alt="Abstract shattered glass art symbolizing broken dialogue">
                    <span class="hero-source">Source: artwithmisslinda.com · Shattered Glass Art</span>
                </div>

                <div class="module-detail-section">
                    <h4>🔍 The Gap Between Literal and Implied</h4>
                    <p class="section-sub-cn cn">字面与潜台词之间的鸿沟</p>
                    <p>Everyday language is full of phrases that don't mean what they say. "Could you pass the salt?" is not a question about ability — it's a request. "I'm fine" often means the opposite. For someone who processes language literally, these invisible layers create a constant translation gap.</p>
                    <p class="cn-text cn">日常语言充满了"言不由衷"的表达。"你能把盐递给我吗？"不是在问有没有能力，而是在提出请求。"我没事"往往意味着相反的意思。对于按字面意思理解语言的人来说，这些看不见的语义层次构成了一道持续存在的翻译鸿沟。</p>
                </div>

                <div class="module-detail-section">
                    <h4>💬 When Politeness Becomes a Wall</h4>
                    <p class="section-sub-cn cn">当客套变成一堵墙</p>
                    <p>Social pleasantries — "Let's grab coffee sometime," "We should hang out" — are often social rituals rather than genuine invitations. For autistic individuals who take words at face value, these rituals can lead to confusion, false expectations, and eventual withdrawal from social interaction. The crack widens not from malice, but from mismatch.</p>
                    <p class="cn-text cn">社交客套——"有空一起喝咖啡""改天约"——往往是社交仪式而非真正的邀约。对于按字面理解的自闭症个体来说，这些仪式会导致困惑、虚假的期待，最终引发社交退缩。裂隙的扩大不是因为恶意，而是因为错位。</p>
                </div>

                <div class="module-detail-section">
                    <h4>🎭 Metaphors as a Foreign Dialect</h4>
                    <p class="section-sub-cn cn">隐喻如同异国方言</p>
                    <p>Idioms like "break a leg," "spill the beans," or "hit the books" make no sense when decoded literally. Research shows that autistic individuals often excel at pattern recognition and systematic thinking, yet struggle with the unpredictable, context-dependent nature of figurative language. This is not a deficit in intelligence — it is a fundamentally different way of processing meaning.</p>
                    <p class="cn-text cn">"祝你好运"（break a leg）、"泄密"（spill the beans）、"用功读书"（hit the books）——这些习语按字面解码毫无意义。研究表明，自闭症个体往往擅长模式识别和系统性思维，但对隐喻这种高度依赖语境的语言形式感到困难。这不是智力缺陷——而是一种根本不同的意义处理方式。</p>
                </div>

                <div class="module-quote-box">
                    <p class="quote-en">"I think in pictures. Words are like a second language to me."</p>
                    <p class="quote-cn cn">"我用画面思考。文字对我来说就像第二语言。"</p>
                    <p class="quote-attr">— Temple Grandin, autism advocate &amp; professor</p>
                    <div class="quote-relate">
                        <p>For many autistic individuals, metaphors and implied meanings are not just confusing — they are like a foreign dialect layered over an already complex language. Grandin's insight reminds us that the "crack" in communication is not a failure of the listener, but a difference in how meaning is built.</p>
                        <p class="cn-text cn">对许多自闭症个体而言，隐喻和言外之意不只是令人困惑——它们像是叠加在复杂语言之上的异国方言。Grandin 的洞察提醒我们：沟通中的"裂隙"不是听者的失败，而是意义构建方式的不同。</p>
                    </div>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
